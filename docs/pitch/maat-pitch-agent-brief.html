The solution

Commit. Pay. Prove. Settle. Remember.

1Commit terms

Vendor, buyer, amount, due time, and metadata on Creditcoin.

2Pay directly

Official Sepolia USDC moves from buyer to vendor.

3Generate proof

Attestcoin returns transaction, Merkle, and continuity evidence.

4Verify receipt

Creditcoin validates and Ma'at decodes exact payment semantics.

5Update policy

Invoice and counterparty trust commit atomically.

The worker transports evidence. It does not decide who paid whom, how much was paid, or which invoice should settle. Compatible proofs can be submitted in batches of up to ten.
